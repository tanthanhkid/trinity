# Privacy

- **Your data stays on your server.** Self-hosted Trinity sends nothing to the maintainers.
- **No AI keys on the server.** Trinity does not call AI models with its own keys. The AI assistant you connect (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini…) runs under your own account, so your conversations follow that provider's terms.
- **Usage stats** (planned) will be anonymous, opt-in and off by default.
- Trinity Cloud customers: data processing terms are in your service agreement.
